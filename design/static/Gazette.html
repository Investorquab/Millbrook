The Millbrook Gazette

Week 2 of Season 3, printed at dawn on day 14

Wage war at the smithy; Gus blames the drought

Smithy workers downed tools on day 15 after Greedy Gus cut their pay to 6 coins, well under the town average of 7.4.

"Wheat costs three times what it did," Gus told the Gazette. "Somebody has to pay for the drought, and it isn't going to be me."

The strike lasted two days. No tools were made, and the weaver and the bakery both ran short.

The bakery held its wages at 8. Two of Gus's smiths have asked to work there instead.

Town Hall opens road jobs

With unemployment at 22%, the Town Hall started paying benefits and hired four residents to repair the river road at 7 coins a day. The money comes from this week's taxes, so the town's 10,000 coins stay in town.

Comings and goings

Old Bram passed away peacefully at 81. His 140 coins went to his niece Mara, the brewer, who says she may open a stall of her own.
